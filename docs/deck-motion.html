Deck motion

Four ways for X's home deck to bring in the next game. Each phone plays a swipe on a loop. Drag any deck yourself to feel how it follows your thumb.

  • It comes from nowhereThe card you can see at the back vanishes, then rises from under the bottom edge of the screen.
  • It travels too farIt crosses most of the screen every time, so even a quick spring reads as floating up.
  • Your thumb doesn't hold itDragging moves the deck at most half a step, so the card lags behind your finger.
Swipe up for the next game, down to go back.
