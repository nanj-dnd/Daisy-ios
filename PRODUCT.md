# Daily Arcade
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
ios
## Stack
Assumption pending user response: native SwiftUI, iOS 17+, no third party runtime dependencies.
## Users
Inferred from the request: people who return to short daily web puzzles.
## Product Purpose
Bring multiple daily puzzle mechanics into one native iOS app. The user explicitly requested Fermi, Minute Cryptic, Krillin, other viral daily games, and a Git repository.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Original puzzle content and branding. Offline daily selection, archive, local progress, streaks and sharing. Krillin is provisionally interpreted as Krillion. No copied site assets, no claimed affiliation, no fabricated multiplayer statistics. Bundled editorial content rotates and must be expanded for launch.
## Open Decisions
Working title Daily Arcade; monetization and App Store developer identity undecided. UI and stack assumptions may be changed by the user.
