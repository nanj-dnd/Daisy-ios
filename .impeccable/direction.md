# X open-fold deck
User-pinned direction supersedes seed f30e3f71 and all earlier proposals.
Mode: Operate, native iOS. App name: X.
WORLD: Joyful, tactile puzzle cards. Pale sky surface, saturated blue front card, mint, coral, yellow, lilac and slate rear leaves. Rounded bold typography. The user explicitly asked for Duolingo-like coloring and elements, not a copy of its characters or assets.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large X, daily progress, centered open accordion of six cards with visible headers. Front card's entire surface is the tap target. Swipe to bring a different leaf forward. One circle at bottom opens the collection; no tab bar or separate Play buttons.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: An open-fold deck with a bottom hinge and staggered top edges. Direct finger translation, subtle perspective and a short settling spring. VoiceOver supports next/previous and activate. Reduce Motion substitutes content changes without 3D movement.
CONTENT: Six playable daily games. Honest conceptual previews, actual local progress, archive, statistics, rules and spoiler-free sharing. Bright color does not imply invented rewards or live population scores.
NATIVE TRANSLATION: All text, diagrams, surfaces and controls are native SwiftUI; no rasterized UI. Native navigation push and one system collection sheet with a segmented section picker. Safe areas and readable Dynamic Type govern layout. Accessibility sizes use a scrollable list. Light and dark appearances supported; no HTML detector applies.
PRESERVE: Open-fold topology, tactile lower rims, bright varied card colors, rounded type, direct interaction and single-circle navigation.
